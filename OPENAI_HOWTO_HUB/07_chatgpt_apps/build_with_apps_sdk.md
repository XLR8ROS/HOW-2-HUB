The term now includes both apps that feature interactive UI and connectors that help you search and reference your information in ChatGPT. We are not removing any existing functionality; previously enabled connectors and company knowledge will continue to work as before.</em></p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>Overview</h1><p>Apps let you work with external tools and information to help you get more done in a ChatGPT conversation. Some apps provide in-chat interactive experiences, while others securely connect to your services and data so ChatGPT can pull relevant context into your responses.<br/><br/>You can use select apps to take actions on your behalf, search and reference information from your data sources, run deep research across multiple sources with citations, or sync content in advance so you have up-to-date information on demand in your workspace’s knowledge base. Available apps are listed in the <a href="https://chatgpt.com/apps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ChatGPT app directory</a>. Browse, discover, and install a wide variety of apps to address your use cases.<br/><br/>Apps are available to all logged-in ChatGPT users, with some exceptions. See the note below.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-yellow"><p><em>Note: some apps may not be available in EEA, GB or Switzerland depending on whether the app partner offers service in the region. Some apps and app functionalities may be only available to Plus/Pro/Business and Enterprise/Edu plans. </em></p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>Connectors are now apps</h1><p><em>To present a single unified experience of connected applications in ChatGPT, we have renamed connectors to apps. See the table below for the new terminology. Functionality has not been affected, and you do not need to reconnect any previously enabled connections.</em></p><div class="intercom-interblocks-table-container"><table><thead><tr><th><span><b>Previous terminology</b></span></th><th><span><b>Current terminology</b></span></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><span>Chat connectors</span></td><td><span>Apps with file search</span></td></tr><tr><td><span>Deep research connectors</span></td><td><span>Apps with deep research</span></td></tr><tr><td><span>Synced connectors</span></td><td><span>Apps with sync</span></td></tr></tbody></table></div></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>App capabilities</h1></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-yellow"><p>Certain app capabilities might be limited to certain ChatGPT plans. For example, an app may have File Search (Chat) capabilities for ChatGPT Enterprise, but only have deep research capability for Plus and Pro.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><p>Apps may have several features to help you accomplish your tasks within ChatGPT. </p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Interactive apps</h3><p>Some apps <a href="https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">include rich, in-chat experiences</a> (for example, interactive cards, maps, or playlists).</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Search</h3><p>Apps can help you search and reference information from connected third-party services, pulling relevant context into your conversation.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Deep research</h3><p>Some apps can be used with deep research for complex, multi-source analysis, with citations back to the originals.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Sync</h3><p>Some apps support syncing to index content in advance for faster responses and improved quality.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Write actions</h3><p>Some apps may be able to take actions (for example, create or update information in a connected service). Availability depends on the app and how it is configured, and our policies require that apps request confirmation from you before proceeding with external actions.<br/><br/>Workspace admins (including Enterprise/Edu admins) can configure which actions an app is allowed to take for their workspace.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>Apps quickstart</h1><ol><li><p>Browse apps from the <a href="https://chatgpt.com/apps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ChatGPT app directory</a>, from <b>Settings &gt; Apps</b>, or from the <b>Apps</b> entry in the sidebar.</p></li><li><p>Select the app you are interested in.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>Connect</b> if available.</p></li><li><p>Complete OAuth and enable sync if required.</p></li><li><p>After the app is connected, invoke it in chat by using @ mentions in your prompt or by selecting <b>+</b> and then <b>More</b> to select the app you want to add.</p></li></ol><p>Read on for more details on setup, including workspace setup options for Business and Enterprise/Edu admins.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>Building your own app</h1><p>In addition to apps available from the <a href="https://chatgpt.com/apps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app directory</a>, you can build your own custom app (formerly “custom connectors”), including to connect ChatGPT to your own tools and internal data.</p><ul><li><p>Build apps using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to let ChatGPT call approved tools and retrieve information from services.</p></li><li><p>If you are on a workspace plan, admins can control whether <a href="https://help.openai.com/articles/12584461" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">custom apps are allowed</a> and how they are rolled out.</p></li><li><p>If you are a developer building an app, the Apps SDK is the recommended way to package and publish app experiences, including apps that use MCP-backed tools. You can get started with building apps by referring to the <a href="https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk?utm_source=chatgpt.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Apps SDK documentation</a>.</p></li></ul><p>You can also submit apps for publication to the ChatGPT app directory. If your app is approved, inclusion in the app directory may make your app experience available to eligible ChatGPT users. See <a href="https://help.openai.com/articles/20001040" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">submitting apps to the ChatGPT app directory</a> for more information.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h1>App directory</h1><p>The <a href="https://chatgpt.com/apps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app directory</a> helps you browse and discover apps in one place. Browse the app directory from <b>Settings &gt; Apps</b>.</p><p>Your app directory view depends on your plan type. If you are on a Business or Enterprise/Edu plan, you can see the apps available for your workspace by selecting the workspace-specific tab. You can use other tabs to browse through app categories such as Lifestyle and Productivity.</p><p>Select each app entry to bring up the app page, which includes information about the app, such as the app’s capabilities. Select <b>Connect</b> to enable the app for use on your account.</p><p>Note: the <b>Connect</b> button for a given app may be greyed out based on geo restrictions, workspace settings, or your plan type. If the button or tooltip says <b>Disabled by admin</b>, ask your workspace admin to enable the app before trying again.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Connect a new app</h3><p>You can add apps from <b>Settings &gt; Apps</b>.</p><ol><li><p>Go to <b>Settings &gt; Apps</b>.</p></li><li><p>Browse the app directory, find the app you are interested in, and select <b>Connect</b>.</p></li><li><p>Complete the app’s login and authorization flow, if applicable.</p></li><li><p>The app is now available for use in ChatGPT conversations.</p></li></ol></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Business and Enterprise/Edu workspace setup</h3><p>Admins and owners can control app availability from <b>Workspace settings &gt; Apps</b>.</p><ul><li><p>Apps are enabled by default for Business plans.</p></li><li><p>Apps are disabled by default for Enterprise/Edu plans.</p></li></ul><p>You can view all available apps from the <b>Directory</b> tab. The <b>Enabled</b> tab shows apps currently enabled for your workspace, and the <b>Drafts</b> tab includes <a href="https://help.openai.com/articles/12584461" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">custom apps being developed</a> for your organization.<br/><br/>The <b>Enabled</b> tab allows you to search and filter for apps and apply actions to a group of apps by selecting them. For example, you can filter for all apps with write actions and disable them as a group.<br/><br/>Workspace users can browse all available apps in the app directory, but they can only connect to apps that have been enabled by their workspace admin.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Business workspace setup for admins and owners</h3><p>Apps are enabled by default for Business plans, including apps with sync. You can manage apps from the <b>Enabled</b> tab.</p><ul><li><p>Select the more options menu (•••) for the app you want to manage.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>App details</b> to review the app.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>Action control</b> to review which actions are available for the app.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>Disable</b> to make the app unavailable for your workspace.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>Manage domains</b> if you want to limit which accounts workspace members can use to connect the app.</p></li></ul><p>You can also use the search and filtering functionality to select and configure multiple apps, so long as the configuration is common to all selected apps.</p></div><div class="intercom-interblocks-callout callout-transparent"><h3>Enterprise/Edu workspace setup for admins and owners</h3><p>Apps are disabled by default for Enterprise/Edu workspaces. You can enable desired apps for your workspace by accessing the <b>Directory</b> tab and selecting <b>Enable</b> in the app listing. Depending on the app, you can configure several app features during the enablement process and select <b>Publish</b> for the changes to take effect.</p><h3>Configure access</h3><p>Choose this option to configure <a href="https://help.openai.com/articles/11750701" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RBAC</a>. By default, the app is available to all users in the workspace, but you can restrict access to specific groups.</p><h3>Configure actions</h3><p>Choose this option to configure the actions an app can perform. Select <b>Action control</b> to review which actions the app can use in your workspace. Admins can choose how the app&#x27;s current actions are handled by allowing all actions, allowing only read actions, or selecting a custom set of actions. If admins select <b>Custom</b>, they can also choose how actions added later are handled by selecting <b>Enable all new actions</b>, <b>Only enable new read actions</b>, or <b>Disable new actions</b>.<br/><br/>For non-sync apps, you can also add parameter constraints to actions. Parameter constraints help control what arguments the model is allowed to send to an app when the action is called. You can apply constraints to all non-object fields in an action, such as strings, numbers, booleans, arrays, and objects with nested properties. When a constraint blocks an action, end users will see a message explaining that the action was blocked due to a workspace configuration and which constraint prevented it.<br/><br/>To add a constraint:</p><ol><li><p>Select <b>Parameter Constraints</b> for the action you want to constrain.</p></li><li><p>Locate the parameter you want to constrain and set the required constraint or filter.</p></li><li><p>Select the save button, such as <b>Save regex</b> for string constraints or <b>Save range</b> for numeric constraints.</p></li></ol><p>If you want to remove the constraint, select <b>Parameter Constraints</b> for the action you want to revert, find the parameter, and then either delete the constraints you imposed or select <b>Reset to default</b>.</p><h3>Manage domains</h3><p>If available, choose this option to limit which accounts workspace members can connect to ChatGPT by restricting connected accounts to an approved set of domains. The list of approved domains is configurable per app.</p><h3>Enable sync</h3><p>Some apps may allow <a href="https://help.openai.com/articles/10847137" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">sync</a>. By default, users have to opt into sync from user settings when they connect the app. Some apps may allow you to enable sync for your entire team.<br/><br/>To enable sync for the app, select the checkbox, then select <b>Publish</b>. If the app supports it, you may see additional options to deploy team-wide or enable self-service.</p><ul><li><p>Select <b>Deploy to your team</b> if available to enable sync for your entire team. Your team members will not need to do any additional setup.</p></li><li><p>Select <b>Self-service setup</b> to allow each team member to set up the app or connector individually from <b>Settings &gt; Apps</b>.</p></li></ul><p>After choosing options and publishing the app, it appears in the <b>Enabled</b> tab.
